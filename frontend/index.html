DAOVAULT is a fully decentralized, community-driven Web3 DeFi platform deployed on BNB Smart Chain (BSC). Members activate their decentralized ID with a single, fixed $300 package in USDT (BEP-20). All distribution logic is encoded into open, published smart contracts that automatically share 40% across 20 generational levels, fund 11-tier leadership milestones, and execute instant payouts directly to your connected wallet.

Activation requires exactly $300.00 USDT (BEP-20), plus a tiny fraction of BNB (typically $0.10 to $0.25) to cover BNB Smart Chain network gas fees. There are zero recurring subscription charges, sign-up fees, or hidden server costs.

For every $300 package activation, exactly 40% ($120.00 USDT) is distributed across 20 upline generations:

  • Level 1 (Direct Apex): 10% ($30.00) • Unlocked immediately with 0 directs.
  • Level 2 (Expansion): 5% ($15.00) • Unlocked with 2 active directs.
  • Levels 3–4 (Mid Expansion): 3% ($9.00 each • $18 total) • Unlocked with 3 & 5 directs.
  • Levels 5–7 (Cluster Depth): 2% ($6.00 each • $18 total) • Unlocked with 7, 9 & 10 directs.
  • Levels 8–20 (Crown Galaxy): 1% ($3.00 each • $39 total) • 15 active directs permanently unlocks all 20 levels.

Once you reach $7,500, further level income pauses until renewal. You never lose your position, your referral genealogy, or your leadership rank. You simply buy a fresh $300 package from your Web3 wallet (Manual Re-Topup) to immediately unlock a new $7,500 earning room with your exact same team.
